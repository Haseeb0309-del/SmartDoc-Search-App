# Nexus Solutions Employee Search Portal — User Manual

Welcome to the Employee Self-Service Search Portal. This guide details how to navigate the portal, manage your query history, filter files, and save offline copies of employee documents.

---

## 🔑 1. Getting Started and Authentication

1. Open the application URL in your browser.
2. If you do not have credentials yet, click **"Need an account? Register here."** and enter your Full Name, corporate email address, and secure password.
3. Log in with your email and password to access the secure knowledge base.

---

## 🔍 2. Performing Searches

The portal utilizes advanced semantic intelligence to retrieve answers from policy documents:

* **Natural Language Queries**: Search using complete sentences, such as:
  * *"How do I connect to the office network from home?"*
  * *"What is the policy for claiming lunch expenses with clients?"*
* **Live Autocomplete Suggestions**: As you type, query suggestions will appear in a dropdown. Clicking a suggestion runs that search immediately.
* **Search History**: Focus on the empty search bar to see your recent search queries for quick reuse.

---

## 🎛️ 3. Filtering and Sorting Results

* **Departmental Filters**: Toggle filters in the left sidebar (**HR**, **IT**, **Finance**) to refine results.
* **Smart Sorting**: Use the **Sort by** dropdown at the top right of search results to toggle between:
  * **Relevance**: Ranked by semantic relevance score.
  * **Date Updated**: Ordered by the document's last updated date.
* **Pagination**: View results in structured pages (10 per page). Use the **Previous** and **Next** buttons at the bottom of the list.

---

## 📄 4. Reading and Downloading Documents

1. Click on any search card to open the **Document Detail View**.
2. Read categorized paragraphs and policy highlights.
3. Click the **"Download .txt"** button at the top or bottom of the document to download an offline text copy to your computer.
